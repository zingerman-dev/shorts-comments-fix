Watching YouTube Shorts on a computer with the comments open? Scroll a little too fast and the panel can show comments from a different short, mix comments of several shorts together, or keep showing the wrong comments every time you come back to that short.

Shorts Comments Fix keeps the comments panel matched to the short you're watching.

How it works
YouTube loads comments in the background. If you move on before they arrive, YouTube puts the late comments into whichever short is open. The extension notices comments that arrive for a short you've already left and loads the comments of the current short instead.

• Also brings back the like and comment buttons when they vanish after a very fast scroll.
• Works on its own, there is nothing to set up.
• Runs only on youtube.com and only touches Shorts.
• Collects no data. It talks only to YouTube, the same way the page itself does.
• If YouTube changes something the extension doesn't recognise, it stays out of the way and YouTube works as usual.
